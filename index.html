The lede

New York native Dan Ackerman is a veteran technology reporter, author, and broadcaster — now shaping the editorial vision at Micro Center, the iconic tech retailer.

From a stint as in-house tech expert on CBS Mornings to leading newsrooms at CNET and Gizmodo, Dan has covered the gadgets, games, and big tech stories for two decades — and told the story of the world's most addictive game in his book The Tetris Effect.

The Work

At Micro Center, Dan shapes the editorial vision and content strategy of the iconic tech retailer — including founding an entirely new consumer tech publication, MC News, with insightful product reviews, practical shopping advice, and the latest industry trends. Key focus areas: AI tools, PC hardware, and tech guidance for professionals, gamers, and creators.

Before that, as Editor-in-Chief of Gizmodo, he helped redefine the publication's editorial voice, driving growth and engagement through sharp news judgment, strategic analysis, and a deep understanding of tech, science, and culture. Earlier still, he led teams at CNET covering everything from laptops and gaming to home entertainment — all while personally testing and reviewing hundreds of products in CNET's New York testing lab.

He also founded and co-hosted the CNET Book Club podcast, interviewing authors including Walter Mosley, Jeff VanderMeer, Neal Stephenson, and Andy Weir. Beyond the desk, he's been a regular TV talking head for years — including several seasons as the in-house tech expert on CBS Mornings.

His writing has appeared in Men's Journal, Maxim, SPIN, WIRED, and Blender, and he has been interviewed by or quoted in The New York Times, USA Today, The Atlantic, and many other publications.
